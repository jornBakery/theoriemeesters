# Visual language

Tokens: `brand/brand.json`. Components: `specifications/components.md`.

---

## Personality (observed)

Educational, trustworthy, exam-focused Dutch retailer; modern e-commerce without playful clutter.

---

## Shape & radius

- UI controls use consistent **`radius-sm` (8px)** on mockup.
- **`radius-pill`** for badges and round carousel controls.
- Logo mixes geometric (cap) and soft curves (book).

---

## Icons

- **Line icons**, navy on light surfaces; white on navy (`comp-usp-trust-strip`).
- Do not mix filled emoji-style sets.

Icon source: **unresolved** (client SVG set or approved library).

---

## Imagery

- Hero: composite product photo (books, mug, laptop UI, phone).
- Products: pack shots / device mockups on white cards.
- No lifestyle people prominent on homepage mockup.

---

## Whitespace

Generous section separation; use `layout-section-padding` / `layout-section-padding-hero` tokens—not ad hoc margins.

---

## Motion

Carousels implied by arrows (products, testimonial). Autoplay / timing **not observed**.
